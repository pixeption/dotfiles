Expand (`plan` skill "Item fields by stage"; copy `cat ~/.claude/skills/plan/templates/item-expand.md`):
once per plan, over all the plan's items (named above), from their approved brief. The brief is
read-only: a finding that would change an Outcome or an exclusion (a Wrong, or what is out of scope)
goes under `Back to owner` with the reason and a proposed edit, never edited, because the approved
brief is the owner's; settle every other AC question yourself. Append below the brief fields: AC (numbered
`<ID>-AC1`…, each checkable, edge and failure cases included, each inside the brief and none
contradicting a Wrong; ids stay stable when expansion splits an item), Reach (every reader, writer,
validator, lowering and caller of what changes, `file` + symbol, each marked changes or reads, plus
the test classes that exercise them), Verify (for each AC the verification that fits it — an
existing test or command where it suffices, a new test only when none does, and the filter of own
and callers' test classes; a new test is plain by default, and one named as Integration states what
a plain test cannot observe; no new test per AC is required), Score (one judged score for how
hard and how big, the `plan` skill's "Scoring"), and a Done when naming the ACs it closes. An item
you would score 8 or 13 is split here into items scored 5 or less, `<ID>.1`, `<ID>.2` …, each with
its own row and section, because no 8 or 13 may reach execution. An item whose Reach cannot be
produced becomes a diagnosis.
