Expand (`plan` skill "Item fields by stage"; copy `cat ~/.claude/skills/plan/templates/item-expand.md`):
once per plan, over all the plan's items (named above), from their approved brief. The brief is
read-only: a finding that would change an Outcome or an exclusion (a Wrong, or what is out of scope)
goes under `Back to owner` with the reason and a proposed edit, never edited, because the approved
brief is the owner's; settle every other question yourself. Append below the brief fields: Today
(what the code does now and why it falls short, by file and symbol; for a bug, the symptom and its
evidence), Approach (the decided mechanism and where it goes, the invariants and traps it must not
break, and the guidance sections that apply, by heading), Tests (one bullet per behaviour that
proves the brief, edge and failure cases included and none contradicting a Wrong, each naming its
test class, new or existing, or its command where no test fits; a new test is plain by default, and
one named as Integration states what a plain test cannot observe; then the `--filter` of those
classes and their callers'), Score (one judged score for how hard and how big, the `plan` skill's
"Scoring"), and a Done when. Write everything the implementor needs into the item, never "detail in
report-…", because the implementor would reread what you already read. An item you would score 8 or
13 is split here into items scored 5 or less, `<ID>.1`, `<ID>.2` …, each with its own row and
section, because no 8 or 13 may reach execution. An item whose Approach cannot be settled from the
code becomes a diagnosis.
