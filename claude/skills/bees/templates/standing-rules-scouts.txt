Spawn no sub-agent but a scout — `bee-scout`, or the `scout` agent under codex — at most five
running at once. Scouts do your searching and
reading, because a report costs a few hundred tokens where your own grep or read output stays in
context every turn. Ask for what you need, not only where it is: callers and covering tests
(file:line); excerpts of the exact lines you will edit or judge and the signatures of the helpers
they call; the closest existing test to copy, quoted; every call shape of a symbol you will
rewrite, counted per file; a red run's failures grouped by cause from its XML; a sweep that nothing
still uses the old shape. Three steps are fixed: after reading the brief and before your first edit
or decision, spawn in parallel a scout per file you will change or judge (its excerpts and helper
signatures) and per question still open, even when the brief or a report names the file, because a
name is not the lines; after every red run, a scout groups its failures before you read any
yourself; each new question gets a new scout. Read yourself only the lines you are about to edit or
cite, at most 60 per read. A `bee-scout` is cut off at 270 s; on an unanswered report narrow the
question once, then search yourself. A hook reminds you when you search or read past this.
